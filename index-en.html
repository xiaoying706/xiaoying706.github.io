COMPANY PROFILE
ABOUT US

Tianjin Pipelinon Energy Technology Co., Ltd. (PIPELINON) is a comprehensive company dedicated to the design, manufacturing, sales, leasing and technical services of pipeline engineering equipment, located at No. 8 Guxing Road, Jingbin Industrial Park, Wuqing District, Tianjin.

Pipelinon's pipeline engineering equipment covers pipeline construction, mountain terrain construction, and pipeline maintenance & repair, making it a professional supplier in the domestic pipeline construction equipment sector with clients across major international pipeline engineering enterprises.

Major Clients: National Pipeline Network, CNPC, Sinopec, CNOOC, China Chemical, China Railway Construction Corp. International markets expanded to the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Russia, Central Asia, Africa and beyond.
Special Equipment License High-Tech Enterprise ISO9001 ISO14001 ISO45001 CE Certified AAA Credit Rating Anti-Corrosion Qualification

◆ Pipeline Construction Equipment

Providing pipe-laying cranes, full hydraulic welding stations, beveling machines, induction heaters, line-up clamps, pipe benders, sandblasting stations, wireless data collectors, wind shelters, pipe cradles and more — 15+ types of specialized pipeline construction equipment.

◆ Mountain Construction Equipment

Specially developed for complex mountain terrain, including YG series tracked pipe carriers, mountain pipe-laying cranes, full-rotation cranes, mountain excavators, mountain welding stations, flexible internal line-up clamps, pipe grippers, and tunnel construction equipment.

◆ Maintenance & Repair Equipment

Providing induction heaters, split-type pipe beveling machines, heating & demagnetizing all-in-one units, pipe end correctors, coating strippers, tracked emergency repair vehicles and more, along with maintenance engineering technical services.
